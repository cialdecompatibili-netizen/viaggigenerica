**A package** is a proposal that is already defined: itinerary, services and conditions are written down. It is convenient if something simple is enough and you want to compare easily.

**A tailor-made trip** starts from your requests: dates, interests, budget, pace. It suits those with particular needs, travelling in a group or wanting a personal itinerary.

## How to decide

- You already have clear dates and budget and want a simple solution: start from the packages.
- You have specific requests or a precise idea: ask for a tailor-made trip.
- You are not sure: write to us and we will tell you what makes sense.
