Before confirming a booking it is worth stopping to check a few points.

- **What is included in the price** and what is not (meals, entrance fees, transfers, luggage).
- **Documents needed** for the destination country: always check the up-to-date requirements and the validity of your document.
- **Change and cancellation conditions**: what happens if you need to change the date or cannot go.
- **Times and routes** of flights or other transport, and the time for connections.
- **Travel insurance**: consider whether you need it and what it covers.
- **Useful contacts** to have with you during the trip.

If something is unclear, ask before confirming: you can write to us through the [contact form]({% include url-ref.html ref="contatti" %}).
