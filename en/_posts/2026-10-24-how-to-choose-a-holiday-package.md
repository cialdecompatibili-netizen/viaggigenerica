A holiday package puts several services together (usually transport and accommodation, sometimes more) in a single proposal. A few questions help you choose well.

## 1. When can you go?

Dates change the choice a lot: if you are flexible you have more options, if your dates are fixed it is better to say so straight away.

## 2. How much do you want to spend?

Set an overall budget, not just the package price: also think about meals, excursions and getting around on the spot.

## 3. What kind of trip are you looking for?

Beach relaxation, art cities, a tour with an itinerary, a cruise? If you are unsure, start from how you want to feel when you get back.

## 4. Who is travelling?

A couple, a family with children and a group of friends have different needs for pace, rooms and services.

## 5. What is included?

Always check what is included and what is not. It is the best way to compare two proposals.
