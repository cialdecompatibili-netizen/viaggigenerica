We organise trips and holidays entirely online. You can choose a proposal from the site or tell us what you are looking for.

## The steps

1. **Choose or tell us.** Find the proposal you like, or write to us with destination, period and number of people.
2. **Receive the quote.** It is free and with no obligation, and it states what is included and what is not.
3. **Confirm.** If you like the proposal, we agree together the steps to book.
4. **Go.** We follow you until departure.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the quote free?** Yes, free and with no obligation.

**Can I ask for a trip I cannot see on the site?** Of course, see the [tailor-made trips]({% include url-ref.html ref="su-misura" %}).

**What is included in the price?** Every proposal states it clearly. If something is unclear, just ask.
